Grinn, in partnership with KYOCERA AVX, Sony Altair, and Skylo, has developed the Terrasat-NTN, a compact and efficient satellite-based reference design utilizing NB-NTN technology to provide reliable connectivity and environmental monitoring for industries such as agriculture, logistics, and energy.
